07FAQ

Questions before you start.

Sonex Dispatch is an AI-powered transportation management system (TMS) for carriers and dispatchers. It combines a dispatch board, load management, AI document parsing, weekly planning and settlements in one platform, with a person approving every load.

AI document parsing reads rate confirmations, bills of lading and proof-of-delivery files, then fills the rate, stops, dates and reference numbers into the load. It flags mismatches and missing signatures so payment is not delayed.

Weekly planning builds a plan for each driver and truck from their lanes, hours of service, home time and minimum rate. It lines up next loads and backhauls before delivery and updates the plan as loads are booked.

No. The AI proposes loads, drafts counteroffers and writes messages, but a person approves every load, rate and message before it is booked or sent.

Sonex Dispatch is for owner-operators, small fleets and freight dispatchers hauling Dry Van, Reefer, Flatbed, Step Deck, Box Truck and Hotshot freight across the 48 contiguous United States.
