Freight dispatch is a service where a dedicated dispatcher finds loads for your truck, negotiates the rate with brokers, and handles rate confirmations, BOLs and invoicing. You approve each load and drive. The dispatcher works load boards and broker relationships around the clock as your back office.

Sonex Logistics LLC is a freight dispatch company based in Cheyenne, Wyoming. It serves owner-operators and small carriers across the 48 contiguous states with load finding, rate negotiation, paperwork, broker relations, IFTA reporting and 24/7 driver support.

No. Sonex never forces dispatch. Your dispatcher presents qualified load options with the rate and details, and you keep final approval on every load, so your truck, schedule, home time and lane preferences always stay under your control.

Sonex dispatch fees are a transparent percentage of gross revenue on loads it books, as set out in your Dispatch Service Agreement. There are no setup fees, and fees apply only to successfully booked loads.

Sonex dispatches six equipment types: Dry Van, Reefer, Flatbed, Step Deck, Box Truck and Hotshot. Dispatch covers regional runs and coast-to-coast routes across the 48 contiguous United States, matched to your equipment, lanes and home time.

Most qualified carriers can start within 24 hours. Onboarding means signing the dispatch agreement, submitting the required documents and a short call about your equipment, lanes and home time, after which your dispatcher begins planning loads.

Sonex is headquartered at 525 Randall Ave, Suite 100, Cheyenne, WY 82001 and dispatches owner-operators and small carriers in all 48 contiguous states, from regional runs to coast-to-coast OTR.
